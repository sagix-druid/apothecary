Lido (LDO) is transforming Ethereum staking through its innovative liquid staking protocol, allowing users to stake ETH while maintaining liquidity via stETH, a rebasing token that accrues rewards and integrates seamlessly across DeFi platforms like Aave, Curve, and Uniswap. Launched in 2020, Lido commands [[tvl]] in TVL and controls more than 30% of all staked ETH, generating $40-60 million annually in protocol revenue directed to a DAO-managed treasury for growth initiatives. With a fixed 1 billion LDO governance token supply, the protocol empowers holders to influence node operations, fees, and multi-chain expansions (including Polygon and Solana), positioning Lido as a cornerstone of decentralized finance infrastructure amid rising competition and regulatory landscapes.

## 📚 The Scholar's Assessment

> Lido dominates Ethereum liquid staking with [[tvl]] in TVL and 30% of all staked ETH. By tokenizing staked ETH as stETH (a rebasing token that accrues staking rewards), Lido unlocked billions in staking capital for DeFi use. The protocol's decentralized node operator network and integration across major DeFi protocols (Aave, Curve, Uniswap) created a network effect that competitors struggle to match. LDO holders govern critical protocol parameters including fee splits and node operator additions. However, unlike fee-burning models, Lido's 5% protocol revenue flows to a DAO-controlled treasury for ecosystem development rather than directly accruing to token holders - a pure governance token model.

## About Lido

Lido, launched in 2020, revolutionized Ethereum staking by introducing liquid staking through stETH tokens. Before Lido, staking ETH meant locking capital with no liquidity - Lido solved this by issuing stETH (a rebasing token representing staked ETH + rewards) that can be used across DeFi. With [[tvl]] in TVL and controlling 30%+ of all staked ETH, Lido became the largest DeFi protocol by TVL. LDO tokens (capped at 1B supply) govern the protocol, controlling critical decisions like node operator selection, fee parameters, and treasury management. The protocol generates ~$40-60M annually (5% of staking rewards), which the DAO actively deploys through massive liquidity mining programs (Curve, Balancer), ecosystem grants, protocol development, audits, and partnerships. This "growth reinvestment" model prioritizes protocol expansion over direct token holder distributions - LDO governance controls when and how to deploy capital, but doesn't receive dividends or benefit from burns.
