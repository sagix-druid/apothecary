<h2>About Optimism</h2>
<p>Optimism, launched in 2021, is an Ethereum Layer 2 scaling solution using Optimistic Rollups technology. It achieves ~3,000-4,000 TPS with fees of ~$0.01-0.05 USD (paid in ETH), making it cost-effective for users and developers. Optimism Mainnet hosts ~$2B in TVL across DeFi protocols like Synthetix and Velodrome. The OP token, launched in 2022 with a 4.3B token cap, powers the Optimism Collective - an innovative dual-governance system featuring the Token House (OP holders) and Citizens' House (for impact evaluation). Optimism is committed to funding public goods through retroactive funding mechanisms.</p>
