These three categories represent the highest-quality stablecoin implementations based on institutional backing and regulatory clarity.

Category 1 includes GENIUS Act compliant fiat-backed tokens (USDC, PYUSD, USD1) with explicit U.S. legal status.

Category 2 encompasses institutional Treasury products (BUIDL, BENJI) managed by BlackRock and Franklin Templeton.

Category 3 contains yield-bearing vehicles (sUSDC, OUSG, USDY, aUSDC) that transform static stablecoins into productive instruments, though their GENIUS Act status remains undefined. These 10 tokens distinguish themselves through regulatory legitimacy, institutional custody, and transparent backing mechanisms.
