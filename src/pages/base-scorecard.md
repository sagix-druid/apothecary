<h2>About Base</h2>
<p>Base, launched by Coinbase in 2023, is an Ethereum Layer 2 network built on Optimism's OP Stack. As a secure, low-cost, builder-friendly blockchain, Base leverages Coinbase's institutional infrastructure and compliance expertise. The network uses ETH for gas fees and has no native token, focusing purely on utility and adoption. Base has quickly grown to over $2B in TVL, hosting major DeFi protocols and consumer applications. Its success represents Coinbase's strategic expansion beyond exchange services into core blockchain infrastructure.</p>
