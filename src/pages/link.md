<h2>About Chainlink</h2>
<p>Chainlink was launched in 2017 by Sergey Nazarov and Steve Ellis as a decentralized oracle network to connect blockchains with off-chain data. The LINK token is used to compensate node operators for providing reliable data feeds, APIs, and computations. Chainlink has evolved to include staking for security, Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP), and support for real-world assets (RWAs). It secures major protocols like Aave and has partnerships with institutions like Tradeweb. As of November 2025, Chainlink's Total Value Secured exceeds $50B, making it a cornerstone of Web3 infrastructure across multiple chains.</p>
