<h2>About Jupiter</h2>
<p>Jupiter launched in October 2021 as Solana's first DEX aggregator, founded by pseudonymous developer "Meow" (co-founder of wBTC). The JUP token debuted January 31, 2024 via the historic "Jupuary" airdrop distributing 1 billion tokens to 955,000 wallets. Jupiter evolved from simple swap aggregation into a full-stack DeFi super app, now offering perpetuals trading (up to 250x leverage), Jupiter Lend (90-95% LTV ratios), JupSOL liquid staking ($1.7B TVL), and upcoming JupUSD stablecoin. The platform processes over 50% of all Solana DEX volume through its proprietary Metis routing algorithm, which aggregates liquidity from 20+ underlying DEXs. In January 2025, Jupiter executed a 3 billion token burn (30% supply reduction) and launched perpetual buyback mechanisms, establishing deflationary tokenomics. As Solana's second-largest network validator, Jupiter contributes directly to blockchain security while serving as the primary gateway for DeFi access across the Solana ecosystem.</p>
