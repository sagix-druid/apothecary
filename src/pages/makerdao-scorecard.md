<h2>About Sky</h2>
<p>Sky launched in 2024 as the rebranded evolution of MakerDAO, founded by Rune Christensen. The SKY governance token was introduced via an upgrade from MKR at a 1:24,000 ratio, enabling enhanced accessibility and participation. Sky offers the USDS stablecoin (upgraded from DAI), Sky Savings Rate for yields, staking for rewards, and modules like Spark for lending. Operating on Ethereum mainnet, Base, and Arbitrum, Sky serves as an onchain capital allocator managing billions across DeFi, CeFi, and real-world assets. The protocol generates substantial revenue through fees and supports governance via SKY holders, positioning it as a bridge between traditional and decentralized finance.</p>
