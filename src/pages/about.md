# Ancient Wisdom for Modern DeFi

Sagix crafts sophisticated portfolio strategies by applying centuries of financial wisdom to today's decentralized finance landscape. We believe that understanding historical patterns provides the deepest insights for navigating current market conditions.

## Our Philosophy: The Druid's Approach

Financial history reveals that the same mechanisms creating wealth and destroying fortunes in 1637, 1837, 1907, and 1994 are repeating in DeFi markets today. While others focus on current trends and technical analysis, Sagix provides the historical context that reveals deeper patterns and more robust strategies.

## The Druid Deep Dive Series

Our flagship content series explores pivotal moments in monetary history - from America's 1837 free banking experiment to Brazil's 1980s hyperinflation crisis - revealing direct parallels to modern DeFi protocols and investment strategies. Each episode demonstrates how historical precedent can guide contemporary portfolio decisions.

## Sophisticated Analysis, Not Speculation

We synthesize research from Federal Reserve economists, academic journals, and authoritative historical sources to provide institutional-grade analysis. Our approach emphasizes quantitative data over popular narratives, helping readers distinguish between genuine opportunities and repeated historical mistakes.

## The Sagix philosophy

Our flagship strategy applies lessons from successful monetary systems throughout history to create diversified DeFi portfolios designed to weather multiple market cycles. Like ancient apothecaries preparing remedies for different conditions, we craft financial strategies for varying market environments.
