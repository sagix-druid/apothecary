The Medici bank was not one firm. It was a chain of legally separate partnerships under one name, so that no branch could sink the house.

The design held when a Milanese merchant sued in Bruges. It did nothing when a branch manager lent twice the branch's capital to a duke.

Episode 15 of The Druid Deep Dive traces both, and reads a Singapore judgment on a cross-chain bridge against it. Full episode on sagix.io, link in bio.
