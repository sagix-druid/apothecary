For more than a century Scotland's money was issued by rival private banks, with no central bank at all.

The notes were cleared weekly and held at par. Between 1809 and 1830 Scottish banks failed at 4.0 per thousand a year against 18.1 in England, though counted back to 1772 the gap disappears, and the episode shows both readings.
