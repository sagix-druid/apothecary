Everyone knows the tulip mania story. The archives know a smaller one.

Prices did spike: ordinary Witte Croonen bulbs rose about 26-fold in January 1637 and lost most of it within a week. But most broken contracts settled for 3.5 to 10 percent of the price, and the historian Anne Goldgar found no bankruptcy caused by tulips.
