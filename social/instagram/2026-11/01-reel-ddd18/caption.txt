Henry VIII never asked Parliament for this tax. He took it out of the coins.

Between 1544 and 1551 the silver in an English penny fell by 83 percent, the mints struck about £4 million of coin, and the Crown kept roughly £1.27 million. Then a 1551 decree halved the face value of the silver coinage, and the people holding it paid a second time.
