The track was real. The traffic was not.

American railroads laid a little over 31,000 miles of line between 1868 and 1873, almost all of it on borrowed money, and by 1870 railroads made up 92.6 percent of the bonds listed on US exchanges. In September 1873 the New York Stock Exchange closed for the first time, and 35.9 percent of the bond market defaulted over the next three years.
