Most tokenized Treasury products can only move between whitelisted wallets. USDY can move freely.

Ondo's USDY holds short-term US Treasuries and passes the interest to holders through a redemption value that rises every day. It is offered under Regulation S to non-US persons, which is what allows permissionless transfers once the initial lockup ends.

Our explainer covers the structure, how the yield follows the Fed, and the risks next to USDC and BUIDL. Full article on sagix.io, link in bio.

USDY is not offered to US persons. Educational content, not financial advice.
