In October 1923 German prices doubled every 3.7 days.

People did not wait for a fix. They moved to dollar-indexed private notes, foreign cash and loans written in rye, coal and gold, until the paper mark was about 7 percent of the money in use. The stabilization that followed was built on what they had already chosen.
